# Pandit Crane Service Official Website

Professional, modern, responsive multi-page website for **Pandit Crane Service** — India's leading heavy crane rental, rigging engineering, and project logistics specialists.

## 🏗️ Overview
- **Heritage**: 20+ years of industrial leadership across Rajasthan, Haryana, Gujarat, and Northern India.
- **Fleet**: 200+ modern, technologically advanced machines ranging from **2 Tons to 700 Tons** (All-Terrain Telescopic Cranes, Crawler Lattice Boom Cranes, Hydra Pick & Carry, Low-Bed Trailers, Forklifts, and Aerial Work Platforms).
- **Safety**: Safe Load Indicator (SLI) telemetry, pre-lift checked rigging gear, and certified operators.

## 🌐 Site Architecture & Pages
- `index.html` — Home page with interactive Crane Capacity & Cost Estimator, live fleet preview, regional hub explorer, verified client ticker, and policies overview.
- `about.html` — Company background, mission/vision, 4 core pillars, benchmark skills, executive team cards, and comprehensive company policies.
- `services.html` (*alias `equipment.html`*) — Complete equipment fleet inventory (Hydraulic, Pick & Carry, Lattice Boom, Trailers, Forklifts, Manlifts) with technical spec PDF downloads.
- `our-services.html` — Specialized industrial domains (Highway Construction, Oil & Gas Refineries, Cement Plants, Wind Power, Solar Plants, Shutdown Project Work).
- `our-clients.html` — Client logo showcase, landmark completed projects (Expressways, Metro, Underpasses), and testimonials.
- `gallery.html` — Field operational photo gallery with filterable categories and interactive JavaScript Lightbox zoom modal.
- `blog.html` — Technical insights, Safe Load Indicator (SLI) guides, equipment selection tips, and category/tag stream.
- `contact.html` (*alias `contacts.html`*) — 24/7 hotline numbers, head & city office addresses, interactive inquiry form, and Google Maps embed.
