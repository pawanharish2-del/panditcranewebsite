Hydraulic Telescopic Mobile Cranes

Our all-terrain hydraulic mobile cranes are engineered for flexible highway mobility and powerful site lifting. Fitted with computerized Safe Load Indicator (SLI) systems, they continuously calculate radius, boom angle, and safe moment to guarantee 100% incident-free lifts.

  • Multi-section telescopic booms reaching up to 100+ meters with fly jib attachments.
  • All-wheel steering & heavy outrigger spread for tight refinery or factory yards.
  • Operated by certified, licensed crane pilots and site rigging supervisors.

Lattice Boom & Crawler Cranes

When lifts demand extreme tonnage and height—such as windmill rotor erection, thermal power plant boilers, refinery columns, or metro bridge girders—our heavy-duty lattice boom crawler cranes deliver supreme stability and load capacity up to 700 Tons.

  • Wide crawler tracks for exceptional stability on unpaved wind farm & solar soils.
  • Modular lattice boom sections configured precisely to project engineering lift diagrams.
  • Accompanied by rigging engineering planning and site ground-bearing assessment.
