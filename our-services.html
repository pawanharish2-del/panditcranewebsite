OUR SERVICES

High Quality Services with Uncompromised Standards of Safety

Backed by certified rigging engineers, Safe Load Indicators (SLI), and 24/7 site supervision across India's key industrial sectors.

Civil Infrastructure

Highway Construction

Heavy girder launching, flyover segment placement, precast RCC railway underpass box erection, and expressway bridge staging (Eastern Peripheral, Agra-Lucknow, Bhavnagar-Talaja).

Petrochemical & Oilfields

Oil and Gas Refineries

Heavy reactor vessel lifting, column erection, pipe rack staging, and furnace maintenance across major refinery complexes in Pachpadra, Barmer, and Panipat with zero-spill protocols.

Industrial Manufacturing

Cement Plant

Heavy lifting for rotary kilns, preheater cyclone towers, ball mill shell positioning, and vertical roller mill maintenance across Chittorgarh and Rajasthan cement belts.

Renewable Energy

Wind Power

Complete wind turbine erection, base section staging, nacelle lifting, and tandem rotor blade hoisting up to 140m hub heights across Barmer and Jaisalmer desert corridors.

Solar Plantation

Solar Plant

Utility-scale solar park logistics, inverter substation placement, heavy transformer offloading, and structural solar farm hardware staging across Rajasthan.

Turnaround & Maintenance

Shutdown Project Work

Time-critical plant shutdown operations, high-speed crane mobilization, replacement of worn industrial components, and 24/7 round-the-clock rigging support.

Based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Fleet of 200+ cranes ranging from 2 tons to 700 tons.
