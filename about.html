Health and Safety Policy

Zero-Accident Rigging & Workforce Protection

People are our most valuable asset and their safety takes precedence over everything. Every effort is made to ensure the safety of our staff and clients. At Pandit Crane Service, we place health and safety of every stakeholder at first place and nothing is more important than that. We take pride in our quality of work and in our services. Our policy strictly follows:

  • Pre-Planned Operations: Operations for site are well planned in advance and are executed keeping in mind safety of each individual at worksite.
  • Safe Load Indicator (SLI) Calibrated: The execution of work in hydraulic crane is done by Safe Load Indicator device which is installed in mobile cranes to alert the operators if the lift is exceeding the safe operating range of machinery.
  • Trained Crew & Driver Support: Helpers and drivers are well aware of Safety measures and the management is always committed to provide the support and resources required for successful completion of work at site.
  • Pre-Checked Lifting Gear: Lifting tools like D-Shackles, hook, ceiling, belts etc. used at site are checked prior to work so that there is ZERO chance of accidents.
  • Constant Client Touch: Our supervisor are very experienced and they are in constant touch with clients. They plan the whole operations and execute the work at the most professional way.
  • Skilled Operator Culture: We are committed to build a culture where we employ high skilled operators and provide them with modern technology devices and young fleet of cranes. Safety of every individual during operations is our number one core value and by working together, we have been able to achieve this goal.

Environmental Policy

Sustainable Development & Clean Energy Projects

We at Pandit Crane Service believe in sustainable development and conserving resources for future generation. We take care to protect and preserve the environment and recognizes environmental protection as a key part of our business performance. We follow these principles to assure best of quality to our clients:

  • We train and educate our workforce about the environmental issues so that our business operations are not affected.
  • We place emphasis on environment and consider it as an integral part of our planning process.
  • We purchase and avail the resources which are best fit for the environment.
  • We are involved in many projects including solar plantation, windmill etc. of government and private entities.
  • We place focus on spill and leakage of hydraulic oil so that fertility and quality of land and soil is unaffected.

We adhere to environmental laws when working at site. We continuously strive to improve our environmental performance, meet environmental goals and maintain a healthy workplace.
